How does TravelOS prevent seat inventory double-booking across multiple OTAs? +

TravelOS implements distributed Redis in-memory locks. When an agent or customer initiates a booking on RedBus, AbhiBus, MakeMyTrip, or your direct counter, the seat is locked instantaneously across all channels with millisecond timeout controls.

Does TravelOS support mandatory government AIS-140 GPS tracking? +

Yes. TravelOS has native telematics listeners supporting AIS-140, Teltonika, and Concox protocols. Location, SOS alerts, speed violations, and unauthorized route detours stream directly to your centralized operations cockpit with sub-5-second latency.

How does the system streamline corporate duty slip approvals and billing? +

Drivers close duty slips on the mobile driver app. The system records start/end GPS odometer readings, automated FASTag toll deductions, and passenger OTP confirmation, instantly generating SAC 9964 GST e-invoices with zero manual billing disputes.

Can TravelOS handle dynamic tariff rules during festive rush periods? +

Yes. Our automated yield management engine adjusts fares in real time based on departure proximity, remaining seat inventory, historical festive demand spikes, and competitor scraping rules to maximize bus operator revenue.

How are customer phone numbers protected from unauthorized driver harvesting? +

Under India's DPDP Act 2023, TravelOS integrates virtual number masking. Drivers and dispatchers connect with passengers via masked VoIP proxy bridges, ensuring passenger phone numbers remain strictly concealed and protected from data harvesting.
