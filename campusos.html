CAMPUS MANAGEMENT FAQS

Frequently asked questions
about CampusOS.

Direct technical answers regarding hardware integrations, accreditation, data migration, and deployment.

Have an institutional architecture query? Speak with our campus tech engineers Email info@ojix.in ↗
Can CampusOS scale across multiple college branches and university departments? +

Yes. CampusOS is engineered with a multi-tenant, multi-entity architecture. A university chancellor or board of trustees can oversee aggregate admissions, financial health, and academic performance across 10+ campuses from a single pane of glass, while campus principals retain strict autonomy over their respective student cohorts.

How does CampusOS integrate with physical biometric and RFID attendance turnstiles? +

CampusOS connects directly with leading biometric fingerprint, facial recognition, and RFID hardware via local gateway controllers and real-time WebSocket APIs. Morning gate punches synchronize in sub-second latency, triggering automated attendance registers and parent SMS notifications.

Does CampusOS support NAAC, NIRF, and AICTE accreditation data compilation? +

Yes. Instead of spending weeks manually collecting faculty research papers, student retention numbers, and examination results, CampusOS maintains an automated institutional data depository. You can export structured SSR (Self Study Report) datasets conforming to NAAC Criteria 1 through 7 with one click.

How does the automated fee management engine handle scholarships and installment plans? +

CampusOS allows finance offices to configure complex fee structures including merit concessions, government quota adjustments, installment schedules, and late fine algorithms. Parents pay through integrated payment gateways (UPI, net banking, cards) with real-time fee receipt generation and direct general ledger reconciliation.

How are student personal records and biometric data protected under the DPDP Act 2023? +

Student and parent personal data is treated with institutional fiduciary care. Biometric hashes and academic records are stored strictly in MeitY-empanelled Tier-IV data centers in India with AES-256 encryption. CampusOS includes native parental consent workflows for minors and data anonymization after graduation in accordance with the DPDP Rules 2025.
