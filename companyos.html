How does CompanyOS integrate with existing biometric attendance machines and turnstiles? +

CompanyOS connects seamlessly with industry-standard biometric turnstiles, RFID sensors, and facial recognition devices via edge gateways. Clock-in data synchronizes within sub-second intervals into central attendance rosters with automated shift rules and zero manual intervention.

Does CompanyOS handle Indian statutory payroll compliance like PF, ESI, and TDS? +

Yes. CompanyOS embeds all statutory calculation slabs for Employee Provident Fund (EPF), ESIC, Professional Tax across Indian states, and dual-regime Income Tax TDS. Monthly payroll generates one-click bank transfer NEFT/RTGS files and automated Form 16 statements.

Can CompanyOS model complex multi-level approval hierarchies for CapEx and expenses? +

Yes. The platform provides a visual delegation of authority matrix where purchase orders, travel expenses, leave requests, and vendor agreements route automatically based on financial thresholds, departmental reporting lines, and escalation timers.

Is our corporate data hosted in India with sovereign compliance? +

Yes. All CompanyOS instances are deployed in Tier-IV MeitY-empanelled data centers in Bengaluru and Mumbai. Your enterprise operations data remains 100% sovereign, adhering strictly to the DPDP Act 2023 with AES-256 encryption at rest.

Can we migrate our existing spreadsheets, legacy ERP, and Tally data into CompanyOS? +

Yes. OJIX provides white-glove engineering migration. We sanitize, structure, and import existing employee masters, historical financial ledgers, inventory SKU lists, and vendor contracts with zero operational downtime.
