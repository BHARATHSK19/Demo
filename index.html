How does OJIX comply with India’s DPDP Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025?

OJIX embeds privacy by design across all platforms. We implement verifiable consent notices available in English and 8th Schedule constitutional languages, itemized purpose specifications, automated data principal rights management (access, correction, erasure), a designated Grievance Redressal Officer reachable at info@ojix.in with 48-hour response guarantees, and 72-hour breach reporting protocols under the DPDP Act 2023 & 2025 Rules.

Where is enterprise data stored and processed in OJIX platforms?

All enterprise client data resides in sovereign cloud regions situated physically within India (MeitY-empanelled cloud data centers). Data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit via TLS 1.3. OJIX enforces strict zero cross-border data leakage and zero third-party data monetization.

What distinguishes an OJIX Operating System from generic SaaS tools?

Generic SaaS tools force businesses to adapt their real-world operations to rigid, fragmented software. OJIX designs and engineers connected operating systems tailored around your organization’s actual roles, approval hierarchies, legal regulations, and operational reality—bringing visibility, repeatable workflows, and executive clarity into one unified environment.

How does OJIX ensure reliable AI workflow automation without hallucinations or security risks?

OJIX builds deterministic, human-in-the-loop AI pipelines. LLMs and machine learning models are used strictly for scoped tasks such as document extraction, classification, and drafting, while business rules, financial computations, and database updates are executed through strict, auditable code with mandatory human approval gates.

Cookie & Local Storage Policy

This Cookie Policy outlines the operational deployment of cookies and local storage on ojix.in by OJIX Engineering & Technology LLP in compliance with India's DPDP Act, 2023 and DPDP Rules, 2025.

What are cookies & browser storage?

Cookies and client-side storage technologies are small cryptographic or text strings placed on your device to maintain secure session integrity, remember your consent states, and facilitate efficient digital platform operation.

Classification of cookies deployed

  • Strictly Necessary (Legitimate Operational Need): Session security tokens, anti-CSRF protections, and your explicit cookie consent state stored in localStorage (ojix_dpdp_cookie_consent). These cookies cannot be turned off as they are critical to fundamental web service delivery.
  • Performance & Diagnostics (Affirmative Consent Required): Anonymized metrics measuring page load times, device viewport categories, and asset delivery performance. These are never shared with third-party advertising brokers or cross-site tracking graphs.

Granular Management & Consent Manager Support

In adherence to Rule 4 of the DPDP Rules 2025, you can review, toggle, or withdraw your cookie consent at any moment using our or through any registered Consent Manager registered with the DPBI.
