What distinguishes an OJIX Operating System from generic SaaS tools?

Generic SaaS tools force businesses to adapt real-world operations to rigid, one-size-fits-all software that produces disconnected silos. OJIX designs and engineers sovereign operating systems tailored around your organization’s actual roles, approval hierarchies, legal regulations, and operational reality—bringing cross-functional visibility, deterministic workflows, and executive clarity into one unified, sovereign environment.

Can OJIX integrate with or migrate legacy ERPs like SAP, Oracle, or Tally?

CATEGORY 03 · AI AUTOMATION

Deterministic AI & Human-in-the-Loop Pipelines

How does OJIX ensure reliable AI workflow automation without hallucinations?

OJIX builds deterministic, human-in-the-loop AI pipelines. LLMs and foundation models are strictly confined to scoped sensory tasks: OCR document extraction, natural language classification, drafting, and semantic clause comparison. All mathematical computations, financial ledgers, policy verifications, and ERP database updates are executed through auditable deterministic code with mandatory human approval gates.

Are our proprietary enterprise data and documents used to train external models?

Never. OJIX deploys private, dedicated model inference instances within your sovereign VPC or air-gapped on-premise infrastructure. Zero client contracts, operational telemetry, financial transactions, or user prompts are ever transmitted to public AI providers or retained for training.

What happens when an automated AI extraction detects ambiguous data?

Our confidence scoring engine automatically flags any field falling below a 99.5% statistical confidence threshold for human triage. The interface highlights the exact source document bounding box with side-by-side verification so the operator can inspect and approve the data with a single keystroke before any system write occurs.

Cookie & Local Storage Policy

This Cookie Policy outlines the operational deployment of cookies and local storage on ojix.in by OJIX Engineering & Technology LLP in compliance with India's DPDP Act, 2023 and DPDP Rules, 2025.

What are cookies & browser storage?

Cookies and client-side storage technologies are small cryptographic or text strings placed on your device to maintain secure session integrity, remember your consent states, and facilitate efficient digital platform operation.

Classification of cookies deployed

  • Strictly Necessary (Legitimate Operational Need): Session security tokens, anti-CSRF protections, and your explicit cookie consent state stored in localStorage (ojix_dpdp_cookie_consent). These cookies cannot be turned off as they are critical to fundamental web service delivery.
  • Performance & Diagnostics (Affirmative Consent Required): Anonymized metrics measuring page load times, device viewport categories, and asset delivery performance. These are never shared with third-party advertising brokers or cross-site tracking graphs.

Granular Management & Consent Manager Support

In adherence to Rule 4 of the DPDP Rules 2025, you can review, toggle, or withdraw your cookie consent at any moment using our or through any registered Consent Manager registered with the DPBI.
